Collect Google Merchant Reviews and display the Google customer reviews badge on your store.

== Description ==

* Settings page under **Settings > Google Reviews** for the merchant ID, badge position/region, and opt-in toggles.
* Shortcodes: `[gm_reviews_badge]` and `[gm_reviews_optin]`.
* Auto-injects the badge site-wide when enabled.
* Auto-fires the opt-in script on the WooCommerce thank-you page when enabled.
